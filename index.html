    About this bank and source limitations

    Each drill is adapted from the referenced exam and schedule, not a randomly generated theory question. Exact exam wording is visible on the original page. Feedback is a schedule-based paraphrase, with alternative accepted answers noted where relevant. The early years contain supporting analysis skills; not every drill is independently an Excellence criterion. Some original scores are redacted, and older resource booklets may be absent. Those questions can be used as evidence/response practice, but cannot substitute for seeing the complete score. Original score links are reference editions; bar numbering and notation may differ. The app does not assess full harmonisation tasks or award NCEA grades.
